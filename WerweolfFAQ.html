How to Play Werewolf

• This Website serves an arbiter or card replacement.
• There are 2 special roles.
• Werewolf is the killer, every night they wake and point to someone to kill.
• Doctor is the savior and part of the villager team, every night they wake and point to someone to spare from death.
• The doctor and the werewolf don't wake up at the same time.
• Villagers must find, through social deduction, who the werewolf is and vote them out.
• Once all werewolves get voted out, the villagers win.
• Once the ratio to werewolves and villagers is 1:1, the werewolf wins.
