Additional Werewolf Roles

• Lone Wolf - A werewolf that would like his fellow werewolf teammates to be voted out to win.
• Witch - A villager that may choose to either save or kill another player once per game.
• Cult Leader - A werewolf that instead of killing, secretly converts everyone into their cult, if successful they win.
• Suicide Bomber - A villager that, when killed or voted out, kills the player to their right and their left.
